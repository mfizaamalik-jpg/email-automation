1. ABOUT THE COMPANY
--------------------
NexaCloud is a cloud-based project management SaaS founded in 2021, headquartered in Bangalore, India, with a distributed team across India, the UK, and Singapore. We serve over 8,000 teams in 40+ countries, ranging from 3-person startups to 500-person enterprises. Our mission is to give teams a single place to plan work, track time, and collaborate without tool-switching.

Core product areas: task and project management, time tracking, Gantt and timeline views, workload planning, team collaboration (comments, mentions, file sharing), and reporting dashboards. Native integrations include Slack, Google Workspace, Microsoft Teams, GitHub, and Zapier.

4. SUPPORT — COMMON ISSUES & FIXES
----------------------------------
LOGIN PROBLEMS
- Password reset: nexacloud.example/reset. If the reset email doesn't arrive within a few minutes, check spam and confirm you're using the account's registered email.
- Account locked after failed attempts: locks clear automatically after 30 minutes, or contact support to unlock sooner.
- SSO login failing (Enterprise): confirm the identity provider metadata is current; expired certificates are the most common cause.

SYNC & DATA
- Data not updating: sync runs every 5 minutes. Force it under Settings > Sync > Sync Now. Clearing the browser cache resolves most stale-data displays.
- Changes lost: check you're not viewing an archived or read-only project.

INTEGRATIONS
- Slack/Google/Teams disconnected: re-authorize under Settings > Integrations. Integration tokens expire every 90 days and must be reconnected.
- GitHub commits not linking: ensure the repository is connected and commit messages reference the task ID (e.g., NX-123).

PROJECTS & EXPORTS
- Missing projects after an upgrade: archived projects are hidden by default — enable "Show archived" in the project list.
- Export failing or timing out: exports over 50,000 rows must be split; apply date filters to reduce the range.
- Report shows no data: confirm the date range and that the project has activity in that window.

BILLING
- Invoice or receipt needed: download from Settings > Billing > Invoices.
- Charged after cancelling: monthly cancellations stop the next cycle; a charge already in progress completes the current period.

Support responds within one business day on Starter/Growth, and within 4 business hours on Enterprise.
